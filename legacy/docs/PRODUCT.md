# PRODUCT

**Register:** product (Operate) for the Explore tab; Read for Story and Data & Method.
**Users:** (1) course graders and TAs checking rubric items fast; (2) NYC planners, transit and housing advocates reading a finding and testing it with filters.
**Purpose:** show whether crash patterns near newly completed affordable housing differ from patterns elsewhere, and let the reader test that themselves.
**Tone:** plain, precise, civic. Honest about limits. No hype, no causal claims.
**Anti-references:** generic SaaS dashboard with identical KPI cards; AI-purple gradients; dark neon "analytics console"; magazine pastiche with serif display.
**Strategic principles:** every number traces to a filter state; every chart states its scope in its title; limits are visible next to findings, not hidden in a footer.
**Constraints:** Python Dash (Plotly), free hosting (512 MB), no map token, WCAG AA, works at 375 px width.
